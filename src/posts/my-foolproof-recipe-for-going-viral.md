After almost a decade of working in digital marketing, here's everything that's worked for me!

## Steps

* Follow people in your niche. Have around a few hundred following
* Scroll for an unholy amount of time. Make sure to interact with videos in the niche you want to break into. Take note of which trends pop up over and over again.
* Copy down the hashtags / captions in said viral videos
* Post once a day on ig and tt
* Respond to three to five comments.
* Post at least two stories and three reposts a day
* The moment you have a video that does even a SLIVER better than normal, make that same video like twenty times but slightly different.
* Rinse and repeat

## Tips

* Don’t do trends more than a week old. Trends should have come out literally in the last two days
* Cut a video off completely unnecessarily and say there’s a part 2 and post within 48 hours
* Three options for how you look:
    * Do some really really good makeup to make yourself smoking hot + a blowout
    * Go childish and do softer makeup + pigtails or braid or something (if you dont mind old creeps)
    * Do some really bad makeup. Like horrendously bad. People will comment
* Don’t do too many hashtags
* You need a hook. Like a really good hook. No dilly dally.
* Review your analytics. Adapt based on it
* Don’t delete videos
* Text your friends
* No VPN on whatever you’re posting
* Absolutely no scheduling tools
* Get friends to follow and interact as much as possible, helps A LOT
* Try to enrage as many people as possible (spelling mistakes, hot takes, etc.)
* If nothing is working you’re either not good at your niche or possibly not pretty enough or ugly enough.
